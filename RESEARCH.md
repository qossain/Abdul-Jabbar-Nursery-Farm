# Nursery catalogue research notes

## Pattoki evidence

The Pakistan Planning Commission's Pattoki nursery cluster report describes the Pattoki cluster as a flower and ornamental nursery hub. It names broad groups including trees, shrubs, palms, creepers, grasses, bonsai, topiary and indoor plants. It also describes flower and ornamental products shipped within Pakistan and to selected countries. The report does not list the verified catalogue of Abdul Jabbar Nursery Farm, and it does not substantiate exports for each plant species in this preview.

The same report says prices depend on plant species and plant or pot size. The preview therefore labels every rupee amount as a sample price and asks the visitor to confirm final details with the nursery.

## Catalogue status

There are seven collections with thirty sample listings each. The names are concept examples for a design review, not a statement of current stock or farm production. Fruit cultivars are illustrative examples relevant to Pakistani home orchards and do not claim that each cultivar is grown or exported from Pattoki.

The preview uses locally stored generated concept images, resized and compressed as WebP. They are representative of plant groups and are not verified farm photographs, current stock evidence or exact depictions of every listed species. Replace them with approved nursery photography before the final catalogue goes live.
