# Client photo listings and indicative prices

These prices are estimates based on other Pakistani sellers, not confirmed prices or availability at Abdul Jabbar Nursery Farm. Confirm each plant, size, variety and price with the owner before launch. Source photos are supplied by the client; the lead photos are AI edited and may differ from actual stock. Some original photos include people, names, logos and contact details; they are displayed at the client's request.
