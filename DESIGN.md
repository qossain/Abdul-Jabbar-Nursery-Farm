# Abdul Jabbar Nursery Farm design direction

## Brand idea

Present a Pattoki nursery with a long local history through confident photography, restrained typography and practical plant information. Keep the page calm and polished. Use the living nursery work as the visual identity.

## Palette and type

- Deep forest: `#173D2D`
- Forest shade: `#10271D`
- Soft paper: `#F6F6F1`
- Quiet stone: `#E8E8DF`
- Leaf sage: `#DCE3D5`
- Muted brass: `#B39A68`

Use Playfair Display for editorial headings and DM Sans for navigation and product details. The provided green nursery logo sets the primary color direction.

## Page order

1. Hero with the saved desktop garden image, the saved mobile rooftop image and two clear contact paths.
2. Nursery history and approved recognition documents.
3. Seven plant collections: indoor, outdoor, fruit, bonsai, cactus, flowering plants and palms.
4. Plant supply and landscape services, with expandable desktop panels and mobile cards.
5. Trending Products with preview listings from several plant collections.
6. Location and contact details, with a floating WhatsApp contact button.
7. Attributed review excerpts immediately above the footer.

## Motion and interaction

Use one short GSAP entrance for the hero copy, smooth collection scrolling, and restrained image movement on hover. Desktop service panels rotate every five seconds when visible and pause during interaction, with a control to stop rotation. On phones, service cards stack as visitors scroll. Review summaries move continuously and pause on hover, focus or tap, while reduced motion keeps them still and scrollable. Respect reduced motion preferences. Make the phone navigation a compact floating capsule with the logo and four dots. Keep the navigation easy to operate with a keyboard.

## Image and content rules

The desktop and mobile hero images and the 100 plant images are locally stored, optimized WebP concept assets from the previous design pass. They are sample visuals, not verified photographs of this nursery's stock. Replace them with owner approved farm photography before launch. The logo is the supplied nursery logo, converted from PNG to WebP. Listing images are marked as representative of their collection, not photographs of the exact plant.

Every collection has thirty example listings. The names and rupee prices are design preview data. They do not confirm that Abdul Jabbar Nursery Farm grows, stocks, sells or exports any specific listed plant. The Pakistan Planning Commission report describes Pattoki as a flower and ornamental plant nursery hub, and reports ornamental products shipped to other cities and selected countries. It does not document this farm's inventory or confirm exports for each species. Keep that distinction clear until the nursery approves a real catalogue.

The five award names and distributor claim are labeled as placeholders for design review. The client count is a nursery estimate requiring confirmation; review counts can change. Selected customer reviews are summarized for clarity and their source excerpts are retained in the review data. Do not present edited summaries as direct quotes or invent customer reviews, stock, plant sizes, delivery promises or current prices.

Keep visible copy free of dash punctuation, hover underlines and arrow marks. Use WhatsApp and telephone enquiries instead of cart or checkout flows.
